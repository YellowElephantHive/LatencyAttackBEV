# Result figures

These PNG files are rendered from slides 14 through 20 of the master's thesis
defense deck. They preserve the slide titles, units, comparison baselines, and
interpretation limits shown during the defense.

The quantitative figures report the GTX 1080 Ti thesis experiments. They do
not contain measurements from the later P100 visualization run.

The clean-input, adversarial-input, and amplified-perturbation figures contain
camera imagery from the [nuScenes dataset](https://www.nuscenes.org/). Motional
makes nuScenes available free of charge strictly for non-commercial use. That
camera imagery remains subject to the nuScenes terms and is not relicensed
under this repository's Apache-2.0 license.
